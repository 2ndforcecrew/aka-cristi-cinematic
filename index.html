02 / The practice

One eye.
Multiple forms.

Photography is content.
Typography is structure.
Motion is behaviour.
AKA.CRISTI is a visual practice moving between fashion imagery, photography, graphic design and art direction. The camera hunts for the exact second light turns ordinary into something unforgettable. The page rebuilds that second into a system a brand can own.
